Tidemark is made by [legal name of the developer], [address] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the Tidemark app.

## The short version

- What you log stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. We cannot see it.
- We do not run servers that receive your health information, and we have no user accounts.
- No advertising, no tracking, no analytics on your health information, and no data sales.
- You can export or delete your data at any time from Settings.

## What you enter

You may enter: your region and age; symptom ratings; how much symptoms affected your day; bleeding; factors such as stress or alcohol; notes; hot flush and night sweat logs; questionnaire answers; medicines and supplements; appointment dates, the name of the clinician, your reason for the visit, and notes about the visit; and questions for your doctor.

This is health information and, in the UK and EU, "special category" data. You choose what to enter, and the app works without notes, names or age.

## Where it is stored

**On your iPhone.** Data is stored in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection. You can also lock the app with Face ID or your passcode.

**In your iCloud.** If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a backup in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account, so you can restore it on a new iPhone. Health fields are stored with CloudKit encryption. This copy is held by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy, in your account. We have no access to it. You can turn iCloud for Tidemark off in iOS Settings; the app will then warn you that your data is not backed up.

**Apple Health (optional).** If you turn on "Save symptoms to Health", the app writes your symptom ratings to Apple Health. Health data is managed by Apple and by you in the Health app. Information from Apple Health is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties by us.

## What we receive

We do not receive your health information.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive from Apple only what Apple provides to all developers, such as anonymous sales reports. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

**Crash reports.** If you agree to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us crash reports. These contain technical information about the device and the crash, not your logged data. [Confirm before publishing that no health data is ever written to logs.]

**Contacting us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to answer you.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The only time your information leaves your devices and your iCloud is when you choose to send it, for example when you share the PDF report or an export file. The recipient you choose is then responsible for it.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export:** Settings › Export all data creates a file with everything you logged (JSON or CSV).
- **Delete:** you can delete single days or everything. Deleted items stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, so you can undo a mistake, and are then removed. Deleting the app removes the data on your iPhone; to remove the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Tidemark.
- **UK and EU:** you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR. Because we do not hold your logged data, you can exercise most of them directly in the app. For anything else, contact us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (UK) or your local supervisory authority.
- **US states:** we do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising. [Check Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370 and Connecticut consumer health data rules with counsel.]

## Children

Tidemark is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
