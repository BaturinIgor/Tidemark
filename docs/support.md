# Tidemark support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to **dawnwell.app+tidemark@gmail.com**.

You can also open the email from the app: Settings › Contact us.

## What helps us answer quickly

- Your iPhone model and iOS version, and the Tidemark version (Settings, at the bottom).
- What you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- If Tidemark shows "can't open your data right now", the code on that screen.

Please don't send your symptom log or other health details. We don't need them to help, and Tidemark never sends them to us.

## Common questions

**Is my data backed up?** Tidemark keeps your data on your iPhone and, encrypted, in your own iCloud. Settings › iCloud backup shows when the last backup happened.

**I got a new iPhone.** Sign in with the same Apple ID and install Tidemark. It finds your backup on first launch and brings your data back.

**I deleted something by mistake.** Settings › Recently deleted keeps deleted items for 30 days.

**How do I cancel the report subscription?** Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Your logged data stays yours after you cancel.

Tidemark records what you report. It does not diagnose, suggest treatment or give medical advice. If you are worried about a symptom, contact your doctor.
