# Tidemark support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to **dawnwell.app+tidemark@gmail.com**.

You can also open the email from the app: Settings › Contact us.

## What helps us answer quickly

- Your iPhone model and iOS version, and the Tidemark version (Settings, at the bottom).
- What you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- If Tidemark shows "can't open your data right now", the code on that screen.

Please don't send your symptom log or other health details. We don't need them to help, and Tidemark never sends them to us.

## Common questions

**Is my data backed up?** Tidemark keeps your data on your iPhone and, encrypted, in your own iCloud. Settings › iCloud backup shows when the last backup happened.

**I got a new iPhone.** Sign in with the same Apple Account and install Tidemark. It finds your backup on first launch and brings your data back.

**I deleted something by mistake.** Settings › Recently deleted keeps deleted items for 30 days.

**What does the subscription add?** The PDF report to print or send, a "since your last visit" comparison page, history and reports over 6 and 12 months, spreadsheet (CSV) export, unlimited symptoms including your own, and extra themes and app icons. Daily logging, history up to 12 weeks, patterns, the report on screen, iCloud backup and JSON export stay free.

**How does the free trial work?** New subscribers get 7 days free. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you are not charged.

**How do I cancel the subscription?** Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Your logged data stays yours after you cancel; your own symptoms and their history are kept.

**How do I turn usage statistics on or off?** Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage in Tidemark. It is off unless you turned it on, and it never includes your health information.

Tidemark records what you report. It does not diagnose, suggest treatment or give medical advice. If you are worried about a symptom, contact your doctor.
