## Description (4000)

Walk into your appointment with the facts, not a blur of bad weeks.

Tidemark helps you keep a simple daily record of perimenopause and menopause symptoms and turns it into a one-page summary you can show your doctor or GP.

LOG IN 30 SECONDS
• Rate up to 8 symptoms you choose: none, mild, moderate or severe
• Note how much symptoms got in the way of your day, any bleeding, and things like stress, alcohol or poor sleep
• Log a hot flush or night sweat with one tap, even from the Lock Screen widget
• Missed a day? Fill in the last three

SEE YOUR OWN PATTERNS
• A day-by-day chart of your symptoms over 4, 8 or 12 weeks
• A cycle calendar with period and spotting days
• Counts of how often a symptom followed things like alcohol or poor sleep
• Your numbers before and after starting a medicine or supplement
• A short weekly summary every Sunday

READY FOR THE APPOINTMENT
• A one-page report: your main symptoms, how often they happened, your Menopause Rating Scale score, cycle, medicines and the questions you want to ask
• Letter size in the US, A4 in the UK, ready to print, email or upload to your patient portal
• After the visit, note what was decided and when to follow up

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Stored on your iPhone and backed up, encrypted, to your own iCloud. We never see it
• No account, no ads, no data sales
• Anonymous usage statistics only if you turn them on, never your health data
• Export everything to a file at any time, and import it back
• Deleted days stay in Recently deleted for 30 days
• Optional Face ID lock and Apple Health sync

FREE, OR MORE WITH A SUBSCRIPTION
Daily logging, history up to 12 weeks, patterns, iCloud backup, export to a file and the on-screen report are free. A subscription adds:
• The PDF report to print, email or upload
• A "since your last visit" page comparing your own numbers before and after a date
• History and reports over 6 and 12 months
• Spreadsheet (CSV) export
• Unlimited symptoms, including ones you name yourself
• Extra themes and app icons

New subscribers get 7 days free, then $39.99 a year or $6.99 a month (prices vary by country). Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the free trial ends, and the subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Tidemark records what you report. It does not diagnose, suggest treatment or give medical advice. If you are worried about a symptom, contact your doctor.

## App Store Connect settings to choose

- Category: Health & Fitness (primary), Medical (secondary) — decide with the lawyer; "Medical" may draw more review questions.
- Age rating: 17+ is not needed; answer "Medical/Treatment Information: Infrequent/Mild" and expect 12+.
- App Privacy: "Data Not Collected". Data in the user's private iCloud database and in Apple Health is not collected by the developer under Apple's definitions. Purchases go through Apple.
- Health and HealthKit: the listing must not mention "HealthKit" by that name in screenshots without the Apple Health guidelines; use "Apple Health".
- Support URL and Privacy Policy URL are required before submission.
